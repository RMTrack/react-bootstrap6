## Overview

`Drawer` (React-Bootstrap's `Offcanvas` before Bootstrap 6) renders Bootstrap 6's Drawer
component: a native
`<dialog class="drawer">` that slides in from an edge of the viewport.

- By default the drawer opens as a modal dialog, so the browser traps focus inside it,
  makes the rest of the page inert and draws the native `::backdrop`. With `scroll` and
  `backdrop={false}` it opens as a non-modal dialog and the page stays interactive.
- Page scrolling is locked with `.dialog-open` on `<html>` unless `scroll` is set.
- The drawer renders in place and stays mounted while closed, so a navbar or a
  responsive breakpoint can show it inline.
- The `ref` points to the `HTMLDialogElement`.
- Focus trapping and restoration are handled by the browser, so the `autoFocus`,
  `enforceFocus` and `restoreFocus` props were removed, along with `backdropClassName`,
  `container` and `manager`.

## Examples

Drawer includes support for a header with a close button and an optional body class
for some initial padding. We suggest that you include drawer headers with dismiss
actions whenever possible, or provide an explicit dismiss action.

<CodeBlock language="jsx" live>

### Responsive

The `responsive` prop shows the drawer's contents inline from the specified breakpoint and
up (`responsive="lg"` renders `.lg:drawer`). Below that breakpoint it behaves as a regular drawer.

<CodeBlock language="jsx" live>
  {Responsive}
</CodeBlock>

### Placement

Drawer supports a few different placements:

- `start` places the drawer on the left of the viewport
- `end` places the drawer on the right of the viewport
- `top` places the drawer on the top of the viewport
- `bottom` places the drawer on the bottom of the viewport

<CodeBlock language="jsx" live noInline>
  {Placement}
</CodeBlock>

### Without animation

Set `animation={false}` to open and close the drawer instantly (`.drawer-instant`).

<CodeBlock language="jsx" live>
  {NoAnimation}
</CodeBlock>

### Backdrop

Scrolling the `<body>` element is disabled when a drawer and its backdrop are
visible. Use the `scroll` prop to toggle `<body>` scrolling and the `backdrop` prop
to toggle the backdrop.

<CodeBlock language="jsx" live noInline>
  {Backdrop}
</CodeBlock>

### Static backdrop

When `backdrop` is set to `static`, the drawer will not close when clicking outside of it, and
plays a short "bounce" animation (`.drawer-static`) instead.

<CodeBlock language="jsx" live>
  {StaticBackdrop}
</CodeBlock>
